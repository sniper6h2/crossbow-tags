# Modrinth listing: Crossbow Tags

## Title

Crossbow Tags

## Summary

Flashes a light-green background behind Piercing crossbows and a light-red one behind Multishot crossbows in your hotbar and inventory, so you always grab the right one mid-fight.

## Description

---

Carrying a Piercing crossbow *and* a Multishot crossbow? In the middle of a fight they look identical. **Crossbow Tags** colour-codes them so you can tell them apart instantly.

| Enchantment | Background |
|---|---|
| 🟩 **Piercing** | flashing **light green** |
| 🟥 **Multishot** | flashing **light red** |

- The background pulses from dim to bright, so it catches your eye without ever disappearing.
- It sits *behind* the item, so the crossbow, glint and durability bar stay fully visible.
- It works in the hotbar and in every inventory and container, and you can switch each one on or off.

## Settings

Press **Right Shift** (or use Mod Menu) to open the **EagerLib** menu. You can change both colours, turn flashing on or off, set the flash speed and the dimmest/brightest opacity, and bind a key to toggle the highlights.

Requires **[EagerLib](https://modrinth.com/mod/eagerlib)**. It's client-side only and works on any server.
